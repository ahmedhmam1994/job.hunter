Features

Everything after "upload CV," automated.

Search, matching, tracking, and follow-ups — built as one loop instead of five separate habits.

◎

CV-matched search across 14 sites

One query fans out to Indeed, Glassdoor, Reed, Adzuna, RemoteOK, Wuzzuf, Bayt and more — including sites behind bot-detection challenges — then ranks results against your parsed CV.

✓

Application tracker

Status pills, per-job notes, and automatic follow-up reminders.

♥

Favorites

Bookmark listings to revisit later.

▤

Response stats

Response rate and interview rate, tracked automatically.

⚙

Your own API keys, your own rules

Drop in free Reed, Adzuna, or Jooble keys, toggle sites on or off, set default search terms, and control how long old data sticks around — all from one Settings tab.

How it works

Three steps, then it runs itself.

01

Upload your CV

PDF or DOCX — Job Hunter parses your skills, years of experience, and seniority automatically.

02

Search once

Pick a query, a country, and a minimum match score. Every enabled site is searched in one pass.

03

Apply and track

One click logs the application, starts a follow-up timer, and keeps notes right on the job.
